Rewrite the prompt below so a capable model gets it right on the first try. Current models do their best work from a clear outcome, the reason behind it, a fixed scope and an observable finish line, with the method left to them.

What the rewrite carries:
- **Goal:** the outcome, not the steps to reach it.
- **Why:** who it serves and what goes wrong without it.
- **Scope:** what is in and what is out. For open work (research, ideas, design), how wide to look.
- **Good looks like:** the quality bar, tied to a real example when the prompt or the conversation has one.
- **Success criteria:** what proves it is finished: a check that passes, or for open work, what a good answer contains.

How to rewrite:
- Keep every fact the prompt holds: each file name, path, link, number, name, quote and constraint, word for word.
- Keep the author's voice and person. The rewrite is still their message.
- Lift step-by-step instructions back to the outcome they serve. Keep a step only when it is clearly a requirement (an order that matters, a tool that must be used), and say why it is required.
- Leave out instructions to verify or double-check; the model already checks its own work.
- Size it to the prompt: a small, clear ask becomes one or two plain sentences with no labels; only a big, vague or open-ended ask gets the labelled lines.
- Where you had to guess something only the author knows, write your best guess followed by "(guess)" so they can fix it before sending.

Reply with the rewritten prompt only: no preface, no notes, no quotation marks around it.
